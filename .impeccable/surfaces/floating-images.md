# Floating supplied media

Mode: Experience behind the existing Read surface. The owner's requested motion
is the focal background addition; do not animate writing or add generic reveals.

SOURCE: The owner's supplied mallory site photos folder: 14 JPEGs, one animated
GIF, and one MOV. The owner explicitly chose to play the clip, not use only a still.
Downloads originals remain unchanged; retain source/output hashes in the app
manifest. All Pinterest placeholder files and their manifest have been removed.

SCOPE: Homepage and legacy alias only. Native Astro responsive WebP delivery for
stills/posters, native small GIF, and silent H.264 MP4 with an inline, muted,
looping video element. No controls, editor/React dependencies, or pointer capture.
All 16 items are decorative/inert/aria-hidden; images have empty alt text. Web
copies are oriented and metadata-stripped; original inputs are not changed.

MOTION: Entire-document coordinates and boundaries, explicitly not the viewport.
Small photographs drift and reflect off document edges and the rectangles of post
blocks with 6px clearance. Respect individual post rectangles and their visible
sparkle-filled gaps without passing through writing. Use
cached geometry refreshed by content/resize observers; scrolling moves the assets
normally without rebasing them. Preserve aspect ratios and the left-anchored cat.

STATES: Reduced motion/data saver freeze the media; hidden tabs and open viewers
stop animation. Offscreen GIF/video use still/paused states; video does not preload.
Autoplay failure leaves a poster, without repeated per-frame playback attempts.
Fit within available gutters; hide if there is insufficient space.
No overlap with writing, no document-height feedback, no accumulated hidden-tab jump.
Clean up observers/listeners and animation frames. Full-document motion remains
separate from the viewport-fixed, input-transparent CRT overlay above everything.

VERIFY: All 16 inputs/web-copy hashes, stripped metadata, GIF frame/timing retention,
actual video playback/muting/offscreen pause, fallback/currentSrc switching, native
variants, moving/static/paused states,
all four document/post collision edges, contiguous-block resolution, full-page
coordinates across scrolling, responsive/no-room states, and desktop/mobile captures.
